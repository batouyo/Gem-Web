Ten IMCL Papers Accepted by MICCAI 2026

Jul  07  2026 , Tue

Recently, the acceptance results for MICCAI 2026, the International Conference on Medical Image Computing and Computer Assisted Intervention, were officially announced. The Intelligent Medical Computing Laboratory (IMCL) has achieved an excellent result with 10 papers accepted by MICCAI 2026, covering research directions such as imaging phenotypes, breast cancer diagnosis, ultrasound analysis, PET-CT prognosis assessment, medical image segmentation and localization, 3D reconstruction, human-AI collaborative agents, and retrieval-augmented graph modeling.

MICCAI is one of the most influential international conferences in medical image computing, computer-assisted diagnosis, computer-assisted intervention, and medical AI. According to the announcement materials, MICCAI 2026 received 4,601 submissions, with 4,402 papers entering the review process and 1,211 papers accepted, corresponding to an acceptance rate of about 28 percent.

This round of acceptances reflects IMCL's sustained efforts in intelligent medical image analysis, multimodal clinical modeling, and trustworthy medical AI. The accepted work spans fundamental representation learning, data-efficient modeling, multimodal diagnosis, tumor analysis, prognosis prediction, and geometry-aware reconstruction, further demonstrating the laboratory's active research presence in the international medical AI community.

The accepted papers highlighted in the shared announcement materials include:

  1. ClinLoop: Human-AI Collaborative Agents for Multimodal Breast Cancer Diagnosis under Incomplete Clinical Data (Paper #2960)
  2. Organ-invariant Tumor Representation Learning via Disentangled Adversarial MAE for Multi-organ Ultrasound Imaging (Paper #2499)
  3. Multi-Annotation Adaptation: A Label-Informed Dynamic Framework for Medical Segmentation and Localization (Paper #2169)
  4. MSQG-3DNet: Multi-Scale Token Selection with Query-Decoder and Gated Fusion for 3D Breast Tumor Classification (Paper #2113)
  5. Beyond the Batch: Momentum-Updated Virtual Cohorts for 3D PET-CT Prognosis (Paper #3495)
  6. From Sparsity to Geometry: Spatial Modeling for 3D Reconstruction from Biplanar Bone X-rays (Paper #3258)
  7. Towards Unified Image-Video Modeling Across B-Mode and Contrast-Enhanced Ultrasound for Multi-Task Analysis (Paper #1246)
  8. ClinRAG-GRAPH: Clinical-prior Retrieval-Augmented Graph Model with Domain Adversarial Learning for Breast pCR Prediction (Paper #1181)
  9. MiGAD: Gut Microbiota-informed Genetic Surrogates for Multimodal Alzheimer's Disease Diagnosis

Congratulations to all faculty members and students involved in these studies. The laboratory will continue advancing high-impact research and promoting the translation of AI technologies into practical medical applications.
