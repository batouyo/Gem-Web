Congratulations to Dr. Yue Sun on Receiving an NSFC Young Scientists Fund (Category C) Project

Aug. 29 2026, Sat.

The Intelligent Medical Computing Laboratory (IMCL, also known as GEMLab) at Macao Polytechnic University is pleased to announce that Dr. Yue Sun has been awarded a 2026 National Natural Science Foundation of China (NSFC) Young Scientists Fund (Category C) project.

Recognition for Early-Career Research

The NSFC recently announced the results of its 2026 centralized review period, covering General Program projects, Young Scientists Fund projects in Categories A, B, and C, the Research Fund for International Scientists, and other funding schemes. Dr. Sun’s successful award recognizes the laboratory’s sustained work and innovative momentum in medical artificial intelligence and intelligent health computing.

The NSFC is an important source of support for fundamental research and original innovation in China. Its Young Scientists Fund is designed to enable early-career researchers to pursue independent investigations into frontier scientific questions and to cultivate promising academic talent. The award affirms Dr. Sun’s research capability and direction, while providing valuable support for the laboratory’s future high-level basic research.

Advancing Intelligent Medicine

In recent years, IMCL has continued to develop research in medical image AI, contactless physiological-signal monitoring, multimodal medical-data analysis, and intelligent clinical decision-making. The laboratory focuses on key scientific questions and practical needs in real clinical settings, using clinical problems to guide research and AI algorithm innovation as a central driver.

Through this approach, IMCL works to deepen the integration of medical data, intelligent algorithms, and clinical applications. The newly funded project will further strengthen the laboratory’s capacity for foundational research and support the growth of young research talent.

Looking Ahead

This achievement marks another step in IMCL’s ongoing efforts to strengthen fundamental research and foster emerging scholars. Looking forward, the laboratory will continue to address frontier challenges in intelligent medicine and precision health, refine its research priorities, deepen interdisciplinary collaboration, and enhance scientific innovation.

Congratulations once again to Dr. Yue Sun on this achievement.
